Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page is a draft template prepared during development. It has not yet been reviewed by a qualified lawyer and should be treated as a placeholder until Sengol Creation completes a full legal review before commercial launch.

1. Nature of Our Products

Sengol Library sells digital e-books. Because digital content is delivered instantly and can be accessed immediately after purchase, e-books are generally non-returnable and non-refundable once access has been granted, except in the specific situations described below.

2. When a Refund May Be Given

We will consider a refund or replacement in the following cases:

  • Duplicate payment: you were charged more than once for the same order.
  • Failed or incomplete delivery: payment was successful but the e-book did not appear in "My Library" and the issue could not be resolved by re-delivery.
  • Corrupted or defective file: the delivered file is unreadable or significantly different from what was described, and a replacement file cannot resolve the issue.
  • Unauthorised transaction: a payment was made without your authorisation, subject to verification.

3. When a Refund Will Not Be Given

  • Change of mind after successfully downloading or accessing the e-book.
  • Dissatisfaction with the book's content, writing style, or subject matter (a book's description and category are shown before purchase to help you decide).
  • Issues caused by the user's own device, software, or internet connection, where the file itself is confirmed to be intact.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us within 7 days of the transaction with your order ID, registered email, and a description of the issue, using the contact details below. We will review the request and respond within a reasonable time.

5. Refund Method & Timeline

Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method used for the transaction. Depending on your bank or payment provider, refunds may take a few business days to reflect in your account after approval.

6. The ₹1 "Support the Sengol Group" Contribution

The ₹1 support contribution is a separate, voluntary payment independent of any book order and is non-refundable, as it is not linked to the delivery of any product or service.
