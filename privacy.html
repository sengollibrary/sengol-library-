We use trusted third-party service providers to operate Sengol Library, including a cloud database/authentication provider (for account and book data) and a payment gateway (for processing payments). 5. Cookies & Local Storage

The website may use your browser's local storage to remember items such as your cart or wishlist for convenience. This is used only to improve your experience and is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

7. Data Retention
